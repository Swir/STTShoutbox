SWIRTV TEAM · VERSION 2.5.2

Your shoutbox.
Three steps to get started.

Independent MyBB chat with coloured usernames, smilies, private conversations and a forum activity bot. Set it up in the admin panel without writing code.

Before you start: back up your forum files and database. You need a MyBB administrator account and access to your hosting files.

01 Upload the files

Extract the ZIP. Open Upload and transfer its contents to your MyBB root directory using your hosting file manager or FTP/SFTP. This is the directory that already contains index.php.

Your forum directory/
├── index.php               ← already here
├── inc/plugins/
│   ├── sttshoutbox.php      ← from the package
│   └── sttshoutbox/         ← entire package folder
└── jscripts/
    ├── sttshoutbox.js       ← from the package
    ├── sttshoutbox.css      ← from the package
    └── sttshoutbox/notify.wav ← bundled sound

Merge directories. Keep all existing inc and jscripts files. Do not upload a directory named Upload or leave just the ZIP on the server.

02 Click Install & Activate

Open Admin CP → Configuration → Plugins → STTShoutbox. Click Install & Activate.

The plugin prepares its own tables and places the chat on the standard MyBB homepage. No additional shoutbox, PluginLibrary or separate bot account is required.

03 Make it yours

Click Appearance & setup beside the plugin.

  • Give your chat a name.
  • Choose Match the forum automatically, Light or Dark.
  • Pick an accent colour and height: 240, 360 or 480 px.
  • Choose whether new messages appear at the top or bottom.
  • Enable private conversations and bot notifications as needed.

Click Save settings, then Open forum. Username colours and graphical smilies come from your forum.

A gentle sound for new messages

The original chime is included: upload jscripts/sttshoutbox/notify.wav with the rest of the package. Use ♪ in the chat header to turn sound on/off. The adjacent sound settings let you adjust the volume and preview it.

Your account's choice is remembered in this browser. Browsers require a click before playing audio; interact with the page after reopening the forum. History, your own messages and bot notices stay silent. A burst of messages produces at most one sound every three seconds.

Choose what your activity bot announces

Open Configuration → Plugins → STTShoutbox → Activity bot, or use the Quick setup link. Thank-you phrases and short replies have independent filters. Both are enabled by default, with a 10-word minimum and replies-only scope.

Edit the phrase list, choose complete-word/phrase matching anywhere or acknowledgements only, include new topics, and optionally exempt links and attachments. The exemption applies only to the length filter and is off by default.

The tester evaluates the current form values, including unsaved changes. It reports a word count and reasons without publishing the sample. Quotes, URLs, code and media do not increase the word count; text inside hide tags is counted.

Filters affect activity notices. Forum posts, unlocking behaviour and conversations are preserved. Previously recorded cards are filtered by default; notifications skipped when a post was created are not backfilled. Full bot guide and examples.

English or Polish?

The chat follows the language chosen in MyBB. Setup follows the ACP language. Polish and English are included, with English fallback. This plugin does not install MyBB's full language packs or translate user messages.

Already using STTShoutbox?

Back up, deactivate, upload all files from Upload, then activate again. Do not uninstall. Hard-refresh the forum. History, settings and your selected appearance are retained. Include the new jscripts/sttshoutbox sound folder. When upgrading from before 2.4, review the filter defaults in Activity bot.

Chat not showing?

Check the forum homepage, plugin activation, uploaded files, enabled setting, administrator preview and group permissions. Quick setup includes troubleshooting help. A custom index template without the standard header variable may need the theme author to place the chat manually.

Requirements and removal

MyBB 1.8, PHP 8.1+, MySQL/MariaDB and mbstring. Uninstall removes settings but preserves conversation history. See the full instructions.
